How it works
Simple to set up.
Runs quietly in the background.

No manual logging, no symptom diaries, no extra effort. FlareWatch works from data you're already collecting, including years of history.

Create an account and connect your wearable

Connect your Garmin account in seconds and FlareWatch pulls in your history straight away. Support for Oura, Fitbit, Polar, Withings, and Whoop is coming soon.

We learn what healthy looks like for you

Everyone's baseline is different. FlareWatch uses your own historical data to establish your personal normal, not a population average.

Get your daily flare risk score

A clear 0–10 score, updated every morning. When it climbs, you get an alert: time to rest, adjust, or reach your care team before the flare peaks.

From the founder
"I was lying in a hospital bed during a serious Crohn's flare, when my smartwatch told me I was fully recovered and ready to train."

That moment, equal parts absurd and clarifying, made it obvious. Wearables weren't built for people like me. The data was there. The hardware was there. But everything about how it was interpreted assumed the user was healthy, optimising performance, chasing a PB. Nobody had built for people managing chronic illness.

Almost three years on, I've lived through multiple severe flares and major surgery. Through all of it, my watch, packed with sensors and collecting my biometrics every few minutes, had given me nothing useful. So I built what I wish had existed.

FlareWatch reads the signals wearables already collect and translates them into something actionable: for people living with IBD, MS, lupus, fibromyalgia, and dozens of other conditions where knowing what's coming makes all the difference.
